Original files are unchanged. URLs, archive members and SHA-256 checksums are in
`metadata/nov14_source_downloads.csv`. Derived Johansen tables and figures retain
this attribution and license; sampling changes are documented in
`metadata/anatomy/NOV14_PROTEOMICS_ATLAS.md`.

Volumes are 5-mm-smoothed. Cortical volumetric data are visualization-only.
Nominal MNI152 geometry does not prove anatomical correspondence to every MNI
atlas variant. Table 2 and volume-derived estimates are not interchangeable.
Neither voxelwise SD nor spatial SD provides the SD of a regional mean.
