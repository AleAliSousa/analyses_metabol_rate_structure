# Tüshaus 2023 Dataset EV1

Publisher workbook, unchanged. DOI: https://doi.org/10.15252/embj.2023114665
Download and checksum: `metadata/nov14_source_downloads.csv`.

Anatomical decisions and source limitations are documented in
`metadata/anatomy/NOV14_PROTEOMICS_ATLAS.md` and
`metadata/anatomy/s1d_tushaus_region_map.csv`. The whole-thalamus label does not
identify a Heiss nucleus. CC is not a global-cortex estimate; CCw is not established
centrum semiovale. Seven other named territories enter metabolic analyses.

The source is one donor with 38 tissue samples. SN's duplicated replicate label
is preserved; distinct sample IDs are used. LFQ/iBAQ values retain their published
log scale. Regional enrichment classes are not cell classes or cell proportions.
